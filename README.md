# E-Commerce Sales & Business Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce business data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, inventory status, order fulfillment, payment patterns, and customer retention.

The project transforms raw transactional data into six interactive dashboards, providing insights into business performance and supporting data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze revenue, orders, and overall business performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and retention patterns.
- Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
- Evaluate inventory status and product demand.
- Analyze order fulfillment, cancellations, and payment methods.
- Segment customers based on purchasing behavior and value.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboard development
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Measures, calculated columns, and business metrics
- **Data Modeling** — Relationships between tables and date analysis
- **Data Visualization** — KPI cards, charts, tables, and comparative analysis

## Data Preparation & Modeling

- Inspected dataset structure, data types, missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.
- Cleaned categorical columns and standardized data formats using Power Query.
- Converted date and numerical columns into appropriate data types.
- Established relationships between relevant tables.
- Created a dedicated date table for time-based analysis.
- Developed DAX measures and calculated columns for business metrics and customer segmentation.

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of business performance through key performance indicators, revenue trends, category and city performance, top products, and order status.

**Focus:** Overall business performance and executive-level decision-making.

### 2. Sales Performance Dashboard
Analyzes revenue, total orders, average order value, month-over-month growth, category-wise revenue, city-wise revenue, and sales trends over time.

**Focus:** Understanding sales trends and identifying revenue growth opportunities.

### 3. Customer Analytics Dashboard
Examines customer counts, new and repeat customers, repeat purchase rate, revenue per customer, customer segments, top customers, and city-wise customer distribution.

**Focus:** Understanding customer behavior and identifying valuable customer groups.

### 4. Product & Inventory Dashboard
Evaluates top- and bottom-performing products, category revenue, quantity sold, stock status, price-versus-quantity relationships, and products requiring attention.

**Focus:** Product performance, demand patterns, and inventory management.

### 5. Orders & Payments Dashboard
Analyzes delivered and cancelled orders, cancellation rate, payment methods, payment-wise revenue, payment-related cancellations, and city-wise cancellation rates.

**Focus:** Order fulfillment efficiency and payment performance.

### 6. Customer Value & Retention Dashboard
Segments customers into one-time, occasional, repeat, and high-value groups. Examines revenue contribution, orders per customer, purchase frequency, and customer value.

**Focus:** Customer retention, purchasing frequency, and long-term customer value.

## Key Business Questions

- How is overall revenue performing over time?
- Which categories, cities, and products contribute most to sales?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- What factors distinguish repeat customers from one-time buyers?
- Which products require further sales or inventory investigation?
- What are the patterns in cancelled orders and payment methods?
- How can customer retention and purchasing frequency be improved?

## Business Recommendations

- Allocate marketing investments toward high-performing categories and products.
- Develop targeted retention campaigns for repeat and high-value customers.
- Investigate underperforming products and identify opportunities to improve sales.
- Review inventory levels using product demand and stock indicators.
- Analyze cancellation patterns to identify potential operational improvements.
- Use customer segmentation to personalize promotions and encourage repeat purchases.

## Key Learnings

- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
- Data modeling and relationship management in Power BI.
- Creating DAX measures and calculated columns.
- Developing interactive dashboards and KPI-driven reports.
- Analyzing customer, product, sales, inventory, and order performance.
- Translating business data into actionable insights and recommendations.
